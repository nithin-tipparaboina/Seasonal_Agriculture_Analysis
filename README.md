# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Abstract

This project presents an analysis of agricultural performance using a dataset containing 4,000 farm records and 28 attributes. The analysis focuses on seasonal performance, crop yield, production, profitability, irrigation methods, water usage, water efficiency, environmental conditions and geographical differences.

Data cleaning was performed to handle missing values while retaining all available records. Exploratory data analysis and visualization techniques were then used to identify patterns and relationships within the dataset.

The results show differences in agricultural performance across seasons, crops, irrigation methods and states. Kharif showed strong overall performance, while Drip irrigation showed higher average yield and profit. The analysis also identified important trade-offs between profitability, yield and water usage.

## Project Objectives

## Key Findings

- Kharif recorded the highest average yield, production and profit among the seasons.
- Zaid recorded the lowest average profit.
- Drip irrigation recorded the highest average yield and profit.
- Rainfed farming recorded the highest water efficiency among irrigation methods.
- Sugarcane recorded the highest average yield and profit but also the highest average water usage.
- Punjab recorded the highest average crop yield and profit among the states.
- Water usage showed a moderate positive relationship with crop yield.
- Rainfall and temperature showed very weak linear relationships with crop yield.
- Higher crop yield was generally associated with higher profit.
